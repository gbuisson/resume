\section*{Experience}
\subsection*{Senior Technical Leader, Splunk: Mar 2026 to Present}
Since joining Splunk within Cisco, I have been working on streaming detection capabilities for cloud based security analytics. I remain closely involved in architecture and implementation, helping evaluate technical options, validate complex workflows and turn emerging requirements into reliable platform capabilities.

My work focuses on distributed systems, real time data processing and the operational foundations needed to run detection workloads reliably at scale.

\subsection*{Senior Technical Leader, Cisco XDR: Oct 2021 to Feb 2026}
As Senior Technical Leader, I guided the evolution of the distributed services and shared platform capabilities behind Cisco XDR. I helped scale the integration architecture to support more than 100 product integrations and platform extensions across multiple product teams. It provided a common foundation for onboarding products, accessing their APIs and delivering consistent capabilities across the platform.

I shaped architectural direction across threat intelligence services, large scale data platforms and event processing systems. My work included evolving Elasticsearch and OpenSearch based infrastructure, strengthening identity and access control patterns, and designing shared capabilities for notifications, audit logging and product integrations. I also extended reusable OAuth based onboarding patterns and helped turn integration specific requirements into capabilities shared across the wider ecosystem. Throughout this period, I remained closely involved in architecture and implementation while helping teams make technical decisions and bring complex projects into production.

\subsection*{Technical Leader: Jun 2020 to Oct 2021}
As Technical Leader, I moved from individual service implementation to platform level architecture across Cisco SecureX and its integration backend. I designed extensible platform capabilities for dashboards, webhooks, contextual navigation, platform observability, service health and uptime monitoring. These capabilities expanded the integration model beyond product connectivity and allowed teams to contribute consistent user facing and operational experiences.

I designed the product onboarding and activation model, using OAuth 2.0 to replace complex manual configuration with guided flows in which products could securely configure their integrations on behalf of customers. I also designed a shared API gateway and proxy capability that centralizes access controls and enables platform applications to reuse configured product integrations across varied standard and integration specific authentication schemes. In parallel, I designed the application of the OAuth 2.0 Device Authorization Flow to unify firewall registration, account linking and SecureX integration into a single customer journey. I guided these initiatives across product and engineering boundaries, translating complex security and identity requirements into reusable platform capabilities.

\subsection*{Clojure Software Engineer: Feb 2016 to Jun 2020}
I helped build the data, identity and integration foundations behind the Cisco Threat Response platform, working primarily in Clojure and ClojureScript. I contributed to a large scale threat intelligence service, its shared data model and the backend responsible for integrating security products into the platform. My work included Elasticsearch based storage and search, data migrations, distributed APIs and shared contracts used across backend and frontend systems.

I helped evolve the threat intelligence service into a secure multi tenant platform with JWT based authentication and data level access control. I also built foundational identity capabilities spanning SAML authentication, signed JWTs and OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect integrations. As the platform expanded, I designed and implemented an extensibility model that allowed security products to contribute investigation, enrichment, response and dashboard capabilities through common module contracts. I also created a reusable integration service for connecting external product APIs while accommodating their different configuration and authentication requirements. I later introduced event driven capabilities using Kafka and asynchronous processing, connecting threat intelligence events to downstream workflows and product integrations.
